# privacy policy

not much to say other than:

- no accounts, no logins, nothing personal required to use this site.
- the only third party involved in running this site is cloudflare, which
  serves the site and provides basic analytics, aggregate pageview stats,
  cookie-less, no personal data collected by me.
- items you save are stored on the server as a list of item ids.
- preferences like alert volume, sound on/off and per-item alert levels are
  kept in your own browser's local storage. they never leave your device.
- price data shown here comes from the public hypixel skyblock bazaar api.
- i do not sell, share or otherwise hand your data to anyone.
